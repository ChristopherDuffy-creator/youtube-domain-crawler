# Expandosaurus 3-Site Validation Pilot

Pilot cohort locked by user: satvic.yoga, petworthy.co, craftsheaven.club.

Goal: prove predicted linked exposure -> real inbound visits -> outbound monetisation clicks -> conversions -> revenue before scaling.

Key rules:
- User is final acquisition gate.
- Preserve production crawler/provider cost controls.
- Do not impersonate former operators or copy protected content/branding.
- Freeze crawler predictions and actual purchase price at acquisition.
- Track sessions, unique visitors, landing path, referrer where available, outbound clicks/CTR, conversions, gross revenue, variable cost and net contribution.
- Review Day 1, 7, 15, 30 and 60.
