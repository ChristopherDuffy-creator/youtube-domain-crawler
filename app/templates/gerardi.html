Equipment guide

Five checks before you buy

Ignore impressive-looking bundles until the practical details work for your home and your training.

Load range

Choose resistance you can control now with enough sensible increments to progress. More weight is not automatically a better starting point.

Available space

Measure the training area and the storage location. Include room to lift, walk around equipment and fold or adjust it safely.

Adjustment

For adjustable equipment, compare how securely it locks and how quickly settings change. Read the manufacturer’s safety guidance.

Stability

Check stated dimensions, maximum user/load guidance and recent reviews for repeated comments about wobble or wear.

Floor protection

Plan for controlled placement rather than dropping weights. A suitable mat may protect flooring, but follow the equipment and flooring guidance.
