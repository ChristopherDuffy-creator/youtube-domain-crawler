How we choose

Reviewed 27 August 2026

We prioritise comfort, everyday usefulness, compact storage and products that solve a clear problem in a home practice. Brands do not pay for ranking, and decorative items are treated as optional rather than essential.

How to choose

Buy for the practice you actually do.

Start with the friction you want to remove, then choose the simplest item that addresses it.

Slipping or hard floors

Begin with a mat. Compare dimensions, material, grip, care instructions and whether the thickness suits your balance.

Uncomfortable seated work

A firm cushion or folded blanket may help you find a more sustainable position. Avoid forcing range of motion.

Restorative shapes

A bolster or folded blanket can support the body. Check measurements and firmness rather than assuming one size fits everyone.

Wanting more depth

A well-chosen book can add structure without filling the room. Choose an accessible introduction or a detailed reference based on your experience.

Difficulty switching off

A consistent time and uncluttered corner matter most. Decorative or scented items are optional and should be used safely with ventilation.

A repeatable beginning

Ten quiet minutes is enough for today.

Keep the first version of your routine deliberately easy. Comfort and repetition make it more likely you will return.

2minutes to arrive and notice your natural breath
5minutes of comfortable, unhurried movement
3minutes resting or sitting quietly before you continue your day
